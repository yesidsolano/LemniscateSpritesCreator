Pixel Artist Guide 🎓

Sprite: A character or object we can program in a video game.

Pixel: A tiny square that helps us build our digital drawing.

Frame: One picture in an animation.

Animation: A sequence of pictures that creates the illusion of movement.

Shortcuts: P Pencil · E Eraser · F Fill · I Picker · Ctrl/Cmd+Z Undo · Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z Redo
